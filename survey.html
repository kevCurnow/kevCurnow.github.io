Data Professional Survey Breakdown

After completing Power BI training, I applied my skills to real survey data from data professionals, focusing on selecting appropriate visualizations for each metric and exploring tools such as gauge and treemap visuals.

  • Download Dashboard

Insights and Takeaways

Exploring patterns in the survey, I created treemaps to see how age and job priorities influence satisfaction levels. In my findings, those focused on salary or work/life balance reported scores 0.43 and 0.81 points below the overall averages. Thirty-somethings were the happiest, while 20–29 year-olds reported the lowest satisfaction levels in both areas. Respondents aged 40–49 also reported lower work/life balance, likely reflecting family demands. Most found breaking into the industry “neither too easy nor too difficult,” though from my perspective, the field is highly competitive.

Next Steps

If I were to explore the responses further, salary would be a great starting point. Is there a threshold that once reached significantly boosts overall happiness? Future research could also investigate advice respondents would give or wish they had received, which might be especially valuable for college students. I would also compare the experiences of those who pivoted into data from another career with those who pursued it from the start.
