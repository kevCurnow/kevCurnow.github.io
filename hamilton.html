The Rise of Hamilton County

As a lifelong resident of Central Indiana, I have long been fascinated by the region’s rapid expansion in the 21st century. This project applies data analytics to better understand and contextualize these trends.

  • Download Dashboard

Insights and Takeaways

It made sense that housing prices dipped after the 2008 economic collapse and surged following the COVID-19 pandemic, but I was surprised to see the average three-bedroom home in my hometown of Fishers was consistently valued lower than in Westfield. This may be due to Westfield’s slow development or a smaller sample size. While I knew Fishers and Carmel were consistently the largest suburbs, it’s remarkable how closely their populations have developed in parallel. Given the data, I’m curious as to why Noblesville was never able to keep up with Fishers and Carmel. It’s also striking that Westfield, despite taking the longest to expand, now has a high school ranked 2nd in the county and 8th in the state. The four main suburbs have outgrown the rest of the county at an astounding rate, and I believe Westfield's population may soon eclipse Noblesville.

Next Steps

Working on this project has sparked my excitement to continue expanding it. I’m eager to explore the factors behind these growth periods and how they might predict the next Fishers or Westfield. I’m also interested in how much room each suburb has to grow and its potential impact on future development.
